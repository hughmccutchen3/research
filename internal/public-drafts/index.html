Public Drafts

Work shared before it is finished. A draft here is still moving — sections may be incomplete, figures may change, and a conclusion may not have settled yet. It is published this way on purpose, so the people reading it can say something while saying it still matters.

How to read a draft here

Each draft carries its own provenance block, the same as a finished paper: what it is, who wrote and edited it, what it does not claim, and where it stands. Read the review status first — it is the difference between an argument being tested and an argument being made.

Nothing here is a Bursera position until it leaves this area. A draft becomes a published piece by moving to its own URL under Research or Training, with a release marker and a version. Until then it is thinking in the open.
