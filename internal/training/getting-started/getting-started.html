Learning from the Interns (+15)

Let the Interns Help You

The firm ran a pilot nobody in leadership wanted to own. Six interns, out of Roxbury Community College in Boston. Everyone agreed they would be a handful. Somebody had to take them, so Beverly gave them to Luke.

Luke had spent twenty years as the person who wrote everything himself, methodical and detailed and a little proud of both. He worked from home now, with two kids and a cat, and he had never managed six of anything.

This was the first day. In theory it was about training the interns. It turned out the training he needed was his own.

Build a good prompt (+25)

At your own pace, draft out a good initial prompt and then hold a minute....

Build Your Seed Prompt as Text

  • Purpose/Goal for understanding and final output
  • What expertise should be used by the tool
  • What kinds of sources
  • Clarify what is complex

DO NOT RUN THE PROMPT STANDALONE

Tell your chat tool to help you build out the prompt

  • tell me what you understand
  • ask me questions where I am unclear
  • challenge my assumptions
  • suggestions for making this better

Raise your hand when you think you have a good prompt

Exercise - Iterate... It's Chat!

Be Conversational

  • "Start by telling me the big picture"
  • "Ask me questions that clarify or refine the prompt"
  • "What am I missing"
  • "What time period are you using"

The Tools Want to Help

  • Demand sources
  • Ask who the thought leaders are and what they think
  • Ask for history and how the topic has evolved
  • Is there anything about your training that we need to compensate for

Push It

  • Tell it what doesn't make sense
  • Tell it when the answers are too long and to summarize
  • Tell it to give you a diagram to explain a big idea!!!!

Finished Early? Try running your prompt in a second tool.

Talking points

This is where mindset makes the difference. Treat the chat tool like your own private team of high energy smarty pants Interns. They are green but they are smart and will work hard for you. Your experience and leadership are the difference between quality and garbage.

LEAD THOSE IN!!!
