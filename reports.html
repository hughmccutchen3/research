Customer Reports

Reports we prepare for a client open from a personal link, sent by email when the report is ready. If you have that link, use it — it opens the folder shared with your organization.

The button below opens the general collection instead. That is material we make available to anyone.

What Google will ask you first

Two screens from Google stand between the button and the reports. Both are normal.

  1. "Bursera Customer File Share (Unverified)." The viewer is a private tool built for our clients, not a public app listed in Google's directory, so Google has not reviewed it. Choose Review permissions to continue.
  2. A permission request. It asks to see only the specific files you choose in the next step. The viewer cannot read anything else in your Google Drive.

After that, pick a report and it opens in the page.

Open Customer Reports

You will need to sign in with a Google account. Any Google account works, including a personal one.
